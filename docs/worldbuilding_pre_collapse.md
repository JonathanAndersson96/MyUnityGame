# Worldbuilding: Pre-Collapse

## Purpose and continuity

This document describes the setting of the game, which takes place during the empire's heyday, before the Collapse. It is a separate game-era reference, not a rewrite of [`worldbuilding_post_collapse.md`](worldbuilding_post_collapse.md). Keep the post-Collapse document intact as its own continuity reference.

When details differ by era, this document governs the game's present. Do not treat events or conditions that arise from the Collapse as if they have already happened.

## Established game-era canon

- **Era:** The empire is flourishing and at the height of its power.
- **Primary danger:** Demons roam the world and threaten the empire and its people.
- **Unbound gods:** There are no unbound gods in the game's present. Do not include unbound gods or threats and conditions that depend on their presence as current game-world facts.
- **World:** The main landmass is Aujurlandar.
- **Genre and tone:** High fantasy with a dark, gritty tone.

## Game direction

- **Visual format:** 2.5D, inspired by the presentation of *Pokemon HeartGold and SoulSilver*: 3D environments, 2D-style characters, and an elevated camera. This is visual inspiration only; do not copy its characters, assets, or mechanics.
- **Movement:** Grid-based.
- **Core activities:** Exploration, battles, and survival.
- **Default challenge structure:** Nuzlocke-style rules are the default. The precise rules and consequences are not yet specified; do not invent them without confirmation.

## Era boundary

The following are not established as present-day facts for this game: the Collapse and its aftermath; a fallen empire; post-Collapse isolation and haunted imperial ruins; gods left unbound by those events; or later historical consequences described in the post-Collapse reference. The demons are the stated threat for this era. Their origin, nature, and relationship to the gods or the empire remain open questions.

## Open design questions

- What demons are, where they come from, and how they behave.
- The player's role within the empire and the immediate reason for the journey.
- The details of grid movement, battle format, and survival systems.
- The exact nuzlocke rules, including encounter limits and what happens when a character is defeated.
- Which locations, peoples, institutions, and parts of the post-Collapse worldbuilding remain unchanged in this earlier era.
